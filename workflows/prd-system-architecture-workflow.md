## 💡 Example Scenarios & Steps
- Create a PRD and system architecture for a multi-tenant SaaS where agencies manage clients, projects, tasks, files, invoices, approvals, comments, and email notifications. It needs role-based access, audit logs, Stripe billing, admin dashboards, and a scalable MVP architecture.
- Design the PRD and technical architecture for an online learning platform where instructors upload courses, students enroll, watch video lessons, complete quizzes, get certificates, and contact support. The MVP should include subscriptions, progress tracking, moderation, and basic analytics.
- Plan a PRD and system design for an ecommerce marketplace connecting local vendors with buyers. Vendors manage products and inventory, buyers place orders, payments are split between vendors and platform, delivery status updates in real time, and admins handle disputes.
- Generate a PRD and architecture for a clinic appointment platform with patient profiles, provider calendars, insurance details, appointment reminders, telehealth links, secure document uploads, and HIPAA-aware data handling.
