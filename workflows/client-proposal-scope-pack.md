They care about trust, professionalism, and getting more consultation requests before tax season. They have logo files and some old website copy, but no formal brand guide.

They need an audit, recommended email sequence, rewritten onboarding emails, and a simple measurement plan. Timeline is 3-4 weeks. They want a clear scope with a lean option and a more complete growth package.
- Client runs a boutique fitness studio launching a new 6-week transformation program. They need campaign messaging, landing page copy, social post copy, and a launch checklist. The target audience is busy professionals aged 30-45. The client wants a premium but friendly tone and needs all copy ready before next month.
