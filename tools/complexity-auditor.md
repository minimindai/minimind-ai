## 🚀 Specialized Variations
This tool has specialized configurations for specific use cases:
- **🧩 [Monolith/Service Modularizer](complexity-auditor-v-monolith-modularizer.md)**: Identify 'God Classes' and suggest clean extraction into autonomous services or modules.
- **📜 [Legacy Code Modernizer](complexity-auditor-v-legacy-modernizer.md)**: Safe blueprint for migrating Var, Callbacks, and Classes into modern Async/Await and Functions.
- **🧠 [Cognitive Load Auditor](complexity-auditor-v-cognitive-load-audit.md)**: Evaluate 'Mental Complexity'—how hard is it for a developer to hold this logic in their head?
- **📐 [Design Pattern Injector](complexity-auditor-v-pattern-injector.md)**: Specifically map messy if-else or switch logic to clean Strategy, State, or Factory patterns.
- **⏱️ [Big O Efficiency Profiler](complexity-auditor-v-big-o-profiler.md)**: Detect hidden performance hazards and O(n^2) traps in nested logic blocks.
- **λ [Functional Purity Expert](complexity-auditor-v-fp-purifier.md)**: Scan for side-effects and mutable state; suggest elegant functional transformations (Piping/Composition).
- **⚡ [Reactive/Async Flow Fixer](complexity-auditor-v-async-flow-fixer.md)**: Simplify 'Promise Hell' and complex pipe chains into readable, maintainable flows.
- **✂️ [DRY Logic Eliminator](complexity-auditor-v-dry-eliminator.md)**: Scan for duplicated patterns/logic and suggest powerful, reusable abstractions.
- **⚛️ [Frontend Cleanliness Audit](complexity-auditor-v-frontend-cleanliness.md)**: Optimize component logic, hooks usage, and state-management patterns for readability.
- **🏢 [Enterprise Logic Auditor](complexity-auditor-v-enterprise-logic-audit.md)**: Ensure business logic adheres to strict enterprise standards: Validation, Error Handling, and Logging.
