## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Condense long articles into key points
- Adjust summary length as needed
- Extract main themes and conclusions
- Process links and pasted text
- Generate executive summaries
- Organize information into bullet points

## 💡 Example Prompts
- "Summarize this article in 3 sentences."
- "Provide a medium-length summary of this research paper."
- "Generate a long summary of this book chapter."
