## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Decode Header and Payload without needing the secret
- Analyze 'exp' and 'nbf' claims to identify expired tokens
- Check for insecure signing algorithms (HS256 vs RS256)
- Visualize user permissions and scopes within the token
- Identify standard vs custom claims and their validity
