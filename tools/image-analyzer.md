## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Identify objects and scenes in images
- Extract text from photos and documents (OCR)
- Analyze image colors and aesthetic mood
- Generate SEO-friendly alt text and captions
- Detect faces and basic sentiment
- Classify images into categories

## 💡 Example Prompts
- "Identify the model and brand of the car in this image."
- "Convert this invoice into a structured JSON format."
- "Extract all text from this receipt and format it as a table."
- "Generate 5 relevant hashtags and a caption for this photo for Instagram."
- "Tell me the main HEX color codes used in this design."
- "Determine if there are any safety hazards visible in this warehouse photo."
- "Describe the lighting and camera angle used in this professional portrait."
