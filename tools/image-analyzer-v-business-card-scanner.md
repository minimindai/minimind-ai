# 💼 Business Card Scanner

Extract contact information from business cards and digitize your network instantly.

This is a specialized variation of the **[Image Analyzer](image-analyzer.md)** tool.

## 🚀 Try It Now
Experience this tool variation with full interactive features directly on MiniMind AI:
👉 **[Run Business Card Scanner on MiniMind AI](https://www.minimindai.com/tools/image-analyzer-v-business-card-scanner)**

---

## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Extract names, titles, and company names
- Capture phone numbers, emails, and websites
- Identify social media handles and physical addresses
- Recognize logos and company branding
- Export contacts directly to vCard or CSV
- Handle vertical and horizontal card designs

## 💡 Example Prompts
- "Extract name, title, company, phone, and email from this business card"
- "Convert this business card into a contact JSON format"
- "Read all contact information from this card"
- "Extract the company name and website from this business card"
