## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Create study guides from notes or books
- Summarize complex educational modules
- Identify key terms and definitions
- Generate review questions and quizzes
- Organize information for better retention
- Support for uploaded textbooks (PDF/TXT)

- "Create a physics study guide on Electromagnetism."
- "Generate biology flashcards for the human circulatory system."
- "Develop a summary of the French Revolution for history study."

- "Generate practice questions for Economics on Supply and Demand."
