# 🏷️ Product Label Reader

Extract ingredients, nutrition facts, and product details with high-precision OCR.

This is a specialized variation of the **[Image Analyzer](image-analyzer.md)** tool.

## 🚀 Try It Now
Experience this tool variation with full interactive features directly on MiniMind AI:
👉 **[Run Product Label Reader on MiniMind AI](https://www.minimindai.com/tools/image-analyzer-v-product-label-reader)**

## 🛠️ Key Capabilities
- Extract ingredients and nutritional information
- Identify manufacturing and expiration dates
- Capture barcodes and serial numbers
- Detect usage instructions and warning labels
- Parse multi-language product labels
- Catalog products in structured inventory lists

## 💡 Example Prompts
- "Extract all ingredients from this product label"
- "Read the nutrition facts table from this package"
- "List all allergens mentioned on this label"
- "Extract the product name, brand, and expiration date"
